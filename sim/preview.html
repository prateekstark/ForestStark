    Same robots in ROS 2 and Gazebo

    This page is the quick, shareable twin. The full software runs in the ros2_ws workspace on ROS 2 Lyrical and Gazebo Jetty: custom Gazebo systems for soil dynamics, and the autonomy stack that would run on the machines.

    Gazebo soil systems (fs_gazebo, C++)

    fs::Terramechanics: Bekker sinkage and compaction resistance, Janosi–Hanamoto slip traction and lateral shear at every wheel or track segment, with a bristle state so machines can stand on slopes. fs::AugerSoil: power- and thrust-limited cutting through the site soil map, refusal on rock, a percussive-pick mode, and the reaction carried into the robot. fs::SwerveDrive for Jaal's four steered wheels.

    Autonomy (fs_autonomy, Python)

    EKF localization (GNSS with tilt-compensated lever arm, odometry, IMU), the planting-head action server, Katar's spacing trigger and boom control, Jaal's fleet manager, navigator and tender protocol, Pahadi's contour planner, articulated steering, stability and traction monitors, and CSV/GeoJSON planting audit logs.

    Run
    ros2 launch fs_bringup katar.launch.py
    ros2 launch fs_bringup jaal.launch.py robots:=3 field:=demo
    ros2 launch fs_bringup pahadi.launch.py slope:=18 side:=downhill

    Add headless:=true rviz:=false to run without windows. Logs go to ~/.ros/fs_logs/.

    Physics is first-order and meant for comparing designs, not for certifying a machine. Soil constants come from published Bekker tables (Wong, Theory of Ground Vehicles). Boring energies, power figures and masses are engineering estimates for these concepts. Every run is seeded, so the same settings give the same result.
